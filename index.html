Frameloom privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Frameloom (Android package com.edit.frameloom) is a video and photo editor made by learnnewhere ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

Your photos, videos and projects stay on your device

Everything you import or create is stored only on your device. We have no servers, no accounts and no sign-up. Nothing you edit is uploaded to us. The AI body effects run entirely on your device.

What we don't do

Frameloom has no analytics, no session recording or replay, no crash reporting and no tracking code of its own. Fonts are built into the app, so no font servers are contacted. We do not send email newsletters.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Four optional features (the Glitch and Light leak effects, removing the watermark and using your own watermark) can be unlocked for 24 hours by watching a rewarded ad. Ads only play when you tap "Watch ad". Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC), which acts as an independent controller. When ads load, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ad loads, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Frameloom under Project > Privacy and legal > Ad privacy choices.

Age

On first launch the app asks for your year of birth. We use it only to choose suitable ads; the app works the same for everyone. Because only the year is asked, borderline answers are put in the younger group:

  • Adult (17 or older): normal ads, after the consent form described above where it applies.
  • Minor (16 or younger): ad requests and Google's consent form are marked as coming from someone under the age of digital consent, so ads are not personalised, and only ads suitable for all ages are shown.
  • Child (13 or younger): everything above, and ad requests are also marked as directed to children, in line with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Only this age group is kept, in local storage on your device. The year of birth itself is discarded immediately and is never stored or sent anywhere. No ads are requested until the question has been answered. The age group is not shared with us; it is passed to Google only as the settings above. To answer again, use "Delete all data on this device" (see below) or reinstall the app.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your projects and imported files (IndexedDB), and small settings such as unlock timers, your age group (adult, minor or child), your watermark and recent colours (local storage). These are needed for the app to work and are never used for tracking.

Deleting your data

Use Project > Privacy and legal > "Delete all data on this device", or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing to delete on our side. To control Google's advertising data, use your device's ad settings (Settings > Google > Ads) or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Contact us at the address below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the policy inside the app's store listing.
